# CODM Squad Hub Constitution

## Purpose

This document establishes the principles and governance of the CODM Squad Hub ecosystem.

---

## Mission

To create a fair, transparent, and professional competitive environment for the Call of Duty: Mobile community.

---

## Core Principles

- Competitive Integrity
- Transparency
- Fair Play
- Respect
- Accountability
- Community First

---

## Rights

Players have the right to:

- Fair competition
- Appeal disciplinary actions
- Accurate rankings
- Contract protection
- Data privacy

Organizations have the right to:

- Recruit players
- Manage teams
- Sell merchandise
- Participate in official events

Tournament Organizers have the right to:

- Host tournaments
- Verify match results
- Report disputes

---

## Responsibilities

Every user must:

- Follow tournament rules
- Respect opponents
- Avoid cheating
- Report violations honestly
- Protect account security

---

## Amendments

The Constitution may only be modified through approved governance procedures.
