# AI Systems

CODM Squad Hub contains multiple AI-powered modules designed to improve competitive performance, moderation, and platform intelligence.

---

# AI Coach

Analyzes:

- Match statistics
- VOD uploads
- Objective play
- Positioning
- Gunfights
- Rotations

Outputs:

- Weekly review
- Performance score
- Personalized drills
- Strengths
- Weaknesses

---

# AI Ranking Predictor

Predicts:

- Player progression
- Team progression
- Seasonal ranking movement

---

# AI Scouting

Used by organizations.

Evaluates:

- Consistency
- Win rate
- K/D
- Objective contribution
- Team chemistry

Produces scouting reports and recruitment recommendations.

---

# AI Contract Advisor

Suggests:

- Contract duration
- Estimated salary ranges
- Buyout values
- Renewal recommendations

---

# AI Tournament Assistant

Assists tournament organizers by:

- Detecting scheduling conflicts
- Validating submissions
- Flagging suspicious match reports
- Monitoring tournament health

---

# AI Anti-Cheat Support

Assists administrators by identifying unusual gameplay patterns that may require manual review.

This system provides indicators only and does not automatically issue bans.

---

# AI Moderation

Helps moderate:

- Chat
- Reports
- Appeals

Flags abusive or inappropriate content for human review.

---

# AI Analytics

Generates insights such as:

- Regional growth trends
- Popular weapons
- Map performance
- Organization statistics
- Tournament participation
