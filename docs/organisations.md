# Organizations and Rosters

Organizations represent esports brands and may own multiple independent competitive rosters:

- T1 — First Team
- T2 — Second Team
- T3 — Academy
- T4 — Development

This organization tier is not a competitive ranking. A T3 academy can outrank another organization's T1 if its verified results are stronger.

Owners receive `*` permission. Additional staff roles include manager, head coach, coach, analyst, scout, media, finance, and custom roles. Sensitive actions require explicit grants such as `roster.manage`, `staff.manage`, `history.manage`, `finance.manage`, `store.manage`, or `governance.manage`.

The system records internal promotions/demotions, contracts, transfer moves, achievements, Hall of Fame entries, reputation changes, and retirement on durable career history. Organization-created achievements remain unverified until a platform administrator verifies them.
