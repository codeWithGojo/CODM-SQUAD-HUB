# Governance Model

CODM Squad Hub uses a multi-level governance structure.

## Level 1

Platform Administrators

Responsible for:

- Platform security
- User management
- System updates

---

## Level 2

Competitive Rules Authority (CRA)

Responsible for:

- Dispute resolution
- Ban reviews
- Appeals
- Rule interpretation

---

## Level 3

Tournament Organizers

Responsible for:

- Tournament operations
- Match verification
- Scheduling
- Reporting

---

## Level 4

Organizations

Responsible for:

- Team management
- Contracts
- Recruitment
- Internal discipline

---

## Level 5

Players

Responsible for:

- Competition
- Sportsmanship
- Accurate reporting
